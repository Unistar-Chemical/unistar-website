3M™ Novec™ Replacement Fluids

3M™ Novec™ Replacement Fluids: Drop-In Alternatives to Avoid Supply Chain Disruptions

August 11, 2025 • Industry News • 3M™ Replacement

With the phase-out of 3M™ Novec™ products, manufacturers are facing increasing supply chain uncertainty and evaluating long-term replacement solutions. Fortunately, high-performance drop-in alternatives are available to help maintain productivity while minimizing process disruption.

Preparing for the Transition

As 3M exits production of Novec™ fluids, manufacturers that rely on these products for precision cleaning, vapor degreasing, thermal management, and fire protection are actively evaluating replacement options. Planning early can help reduce supply chain risk while ensuring production continues without unnecessary downtime.

Drop-In Replacement Solutions

Unistar Chemical offers a complete family of replacement fluids designed to integrate into existing processes with minimal disruption. These products provide comparable performance and physical properties while helping customers maintain reliability and long-term availability.
