Trans-DCE as a Replacement

Trans-DCE as a Replacement for Traditional Chlorinated Solvents

August 22, 2025 • Industry News • Precision Cleaning

As regulatory pressure continues to increase on trichloroethylene (TCE) and n-propyl bromide (nPB), manufacturers are evaluating safer, more sustainable alternatives. Trans-1,2-dichloroethylene (Trans-DCE) has become a practical solution for many precision cleaning and vapor degreasing applications, offering strong cleaning performance while helping companies prepare for evolving compliance requirements.

Why Traditional Solvents Are Being Replaced

For decades, TCE and nPB were widely used throughout aerospace, automotive, electronics, and industrial manufacturing because of their excellent ability to remove oils, greases, waxes, and other contaminants.

However, growing concerns surrounding worker safety, environmental impact, and regulatory compliance have significantly changed the outlook for these solvents.

TCE has been identified as a human carcinogen, while nPB has been associated with reproductive toxicity and other health concerns. Increasing regulations have encouraged manufacturers to evaluate replacement chemistries that better align with modern environmental and workplace safety standards.

What Is Trans-DCE?

Trans-1,2-dichloroethylene (Trans-DCE) is a chlorinated solvent commonly used in industrial cleaning applications. It is a clear, volatile liquid with a relatively low boiling point of approximately 48°C, making it well suited for fast drying and vapor degreasing processes.

Compared to many legacy chlorinated solvents, Trans-DCE provides an effective balance of cleaning performance, evaporation rate, and process efficiency for precision manufacturing environments.

How Trans-DCE Compares to TCE and nPB

One of the primary advantages of Trans-DCE is its ability to maintain excellent cleaning performance while reducing many of the concerns associated with traditional chlorinated solvents.

  • Fast evaporation for shorter production cycles
  • Excellent solvency for oils, greases, and machining residues
  • Suitable for vapor degreasing applications
  • Strong compatibility with many industrial cleaning processes
  • Lower regulatory pressure than many legacy solvents

Industrial Applications

Trans-DCE is used throughout numerous manufacturing industries where high-performance cleaning is required.

  • Precision cleaning
  • Vapor degreasing
  • Electronics manufacturing
  • Automotive manufacturing
  • Aerospace component cleaning
  • Metal finishing

Its fast drying characteristics and excellent cleaning capability make it particularly valuable when processing complex or delicate components that cannot tolerate residue. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Important Considerations Before Switching

Although Trans-DCE offers many advantages over older solvent systems, every manufacturing process is unique.

Before implementing a replacement solvent, companies should evaluate:

  • Material compatibility
  • Cleaning performance requirements
  • Equipment compatibility
  • Worker safety procedures
  • Ventilation and handling requirements
  • Environmental and regulatory objectives

Conducting process evaluations before changing chemistries helps ensure successful implementation while minimizing production interruptions.
