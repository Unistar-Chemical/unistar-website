Alternatives to 3M™ Novec™ 7100, 7200, and 7300

Alternatives to 3M™ Novec™ 7100, 7200, and 7300

April 10, 2025 • Product Solutions • 3M™ Replacement

As 3M discontinues its Novec™ product line, companies that rely on these specialty fluids for precision cleaning, thermal management, and other industrial applications need reliable replacement options. Unisolv-7100, Unisolv-7200, and Unisolv-7300 provide alternative pathways designed to support a smooth transition.

Why Make the Switch to Unisolv?

Manufacturers have relied on 3M™ Novec™ engineered fluids for applications requiring nonflammable chemistry, low surface tension, fast evaporation, and compatibility with sensitive materials.

With the Novec™ product line being discontinued, long-term availability has become a growing concern. Companies that wait too long to qualify replacement fluids may face limited inventory, increased costs, and unexpected supply chain disruption.

Unistar Chemical developed the Unisolv product family to provide dependable alternatives for customers currently using Novec™ 7100, 7200, and 7300.

Seamless Compatibility with Existing Systems

Unisolv-7100, Unisolv-7200, and Unisolv-7300 are designed to provide comparable physical properties and process performance to their respective Novec™ counterparts.

Depending on the application, these fluids may be evaluated for use in existing equipment and production systems without requiring major changes to operating procedures.

This can help manufacturers reduce transition time, limit production interruptions, and avoid unnecessary equipment investments.

Long-Term Availability and Supply Stability

The discontinuation of the Novec™ product line has created uncertainty for manufacturers that depend on these fluids for critical production processes.

Unisolv products are supported through alternative manufacturing and sourcing channels intended to provide customers with dependable long-term availability.

Establishing a replacement supply chain early can help companies avoid emergency purchasing decisions, inconsistent inventory, and costly interruptions later.

Environmental and Safety Considerations

Chemical selection increasingly requires manufacturers to balance process performance with workplace safety and environmental goals.

Unisolv-7100, Unisolv-7200, and Unisolv-7300 are nonflammable specialty fluids with zero ozone depletion potential and lower global warming potential than many older fluorinated chemistries.

Companies should review the applicable Safety Data Sheet, Technical Data Sheet, regulatory requirements, and internal sustainability policies before approving any replacement fluid.

Trusted Performance Across Critical Applications

Unisolv fluids support a range of industrial processes where controlled evaporation, low residue, material compatibility, and consistent performance are important.

Typical application pathways include:

  • Precision component cleaning
  • Electronics manufacturing
  • Vapor degreasing and rinsing
  • Heat transfer and thermal management
  • Carrier solvent applications
  • Specialty coating and lubricant deposition
  • Aerospace and medical component processing

Because every process has different contamination, equipment, and material-compatibility requirements, customers should complete application testing before making a final product change.

Competitive Pricing Without Sacrificing Quality

Product availability is only one part of a successful transition. Manufacturers must also consider pricing, quality consistency, technical documentation, lead times, and ongoing supplier support.

Unistar Chemical works with established manufacturing partners to provide competitive pricing, consistent product quality, and reliable logistics.

Our team can also provide Safety Data Sheets, Technical Data Sheets, product samples, and application guidance to support customer qualification programs.

Preparing for a Successful Transition

Companies currently using Novec™ 7100, 7200, or 7300 should begin documenting their existing process requirements before selecting a replacement.

A product evaluation should consider:

  • The current Novec™ product being used
  • Required boiling point and evaporation rate
  • Cleaning or thermal performance requirements
  • Compatibility with plastics, metals, elastomers, and coatings
  • Existing equipment and operating conditions
  • Annual product volume and delivery requirements
  • Regulatory and sustainability objectives

Early qualification gives your organization time to compare products, conduct controlled trials, update documentation, and transition before remaining Novec™ inventory becomes difficult to obtain.

The Right Choice for a Changing Industry

The specialty chemical market is changing, but manufacturers do not have to sacrifice process reliability or product quality.

Unisolv-7100, Unisolv-7200, and Unisolv-7300 provide practical replacement pathways for companies preparing for the discontinuation of 3M™ Novec™ engineered fluids.
