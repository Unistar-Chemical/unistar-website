Electronics Manufacturing

Precision Cleaning Solutions for Critical Components

Supporting High-Performance Electronics Manufacturing

Electronics manufacturers require precision cleaning solutions that remove flux residues, oils, particulates, and manufacturing contaminants without compromising sensitive components. Unistar Chemical provides specialty fluids designed to support electronics production, PCB assembly, precision manufacturing, and component cleaning applications.

Reliable Performance for Modern Manufacturing

Our application-focused products help manufacturers improve cleaning performance, reduce residue, maintain product quality, and evaluate replacement solutions for evolving environmental and regulatory requirements.

Typical Applications

Our specialty fluids support a wide variety of electronics manufacturing processes.

PCB Assembly Cleaning

Remove flux residues and contaminants following soldering and assembly operations.

Precision Component Cleaning

Clean delicate electronic components while maintaining high levels of performance and reliability.

Vapor Degreasing

Remove machining oils, greases, and production residues from precision components.

Replacement Evaluations

Identify replacement fluids for legacy products while minimizing process changes.
