HFE-347

High-Purity Hydrofluoroether Specialty Fluid

HFE-347 is a high-purity hydrofluoroether specialty fluid composed of 1,1,2,2-tetrafluoroethyl-2,2,2-trifluoroethyl ether. It is a clear, colorless liquid with an ethereal odor and is intended for controlled industrial or professional use.

Product Overview

HFE-347 contains between 99 and 100 percent 1,1,2,2-tetrafluoroethyl-2,2,2-trifluoroethyl ether. It is a clear, colorless liquid that is insoluble in water and soluble in acetone and ether.

The product should be handled in a well-ventilated area or fume hood. Exposure to vapors may cause drowsiness or dizziness, and users should review the current Safety Data Sheet before handling, storing, or using the product.

Safety and Handling Information

HFE-347 carries the signal word “Warning” and may cause drowsiness or dizziness through vapor exposure. Avoid breathing vapors, mist, or spray, and use the product only outdoors or in a well-ventilated area.

Keep the container tightly closed and store it in a cool, well-ventilated location away from direct sunlight or heat sources. Appropriate eye protection, chemical-resistant gloves, protective clothing, and respiratory protection should be selected based on a workplace risk assessment.
