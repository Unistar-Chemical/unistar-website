Trans-DCE

High-Purity Solvent for Industrial Cleaning Applications

Trans-DCE, also known as trans-1,2-dichloroethylene, is a high-purity chlorinated hydrocarbon solvent used in industrial cleaning, precision cleaning, component cleaning, and vapor degreasing applications. Its low boiling point and rapid evaporation characteristics support efficient cleaning and drying in controlled industrial processes.

Product Overview

Trans-DCE is a clear, colorless liquid containing more than 99 percent trans-1,2-dichloroethylene. Its low boiling point, high vapor pressure, and effective solvency make it suitable for selected industrial cleaning and degreasing processes.

Trans-DCE is highly flammable and must be handled using appropriate ventilation, ignition-control measures, protective equipment, and storage procedures. Always review the current Safety Data Sheet before handling or using this product.

Safety and Handling Information

Trans-DCE is classified as a highly flammable liquid and vapor. Keep it away from heat, sparks, open flames, hot surfaces, and other ignition sources. Containers should remain tightly closed and the product should only be used outdoors or in a well-ventilated area.

Appropriate chemical-resistant gloves, eye protection, face protection, ventilation, and respiratory protection should be selected according to the conditions of use. Avoid breathing vapors and prevent release to the environment.
