Product Overview

Unisolv-1234yf consists of 2,3,3,3-tetrafluoropropene and is supplied as a liquefied gas. The product is also commonly identified as R-1234yf, HFO-1234yf, HFC-1234yf, and 2,3,3,3-tetrafluoroprop-1-ene.

Safety and Handling

Unisolv-1234yf is classified as an extremely flammable gas and a liquefied gas under pressure. Containers may rupture or explode if heated. Keep the product away from heat, sparks, open flames, hot surfaces, and other ignition sources.

Adequate Ventilation

Use only with adequate ventilation. Released gas may displace oxygen and cause rapid suffocation.

Ignition Control

Keep away from sparks, flames, hot surfaces, and other ignition sources. Do not smoke near the product.

Cold-Contact Protection

Contact with rapidly evaporating liquid may freeze tissue and cause burns similar to frostbite.

Pressure-Rated Equipment

Use equipment rated for cylinder pressure and close the valve after each use and when the container is empty.

Storage Information

Store Unisolv-1234yf in a segregated, approved, dry, cool, and well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight and incompatible materials. Eliminate all ignition sources and keep containers tightly closed until ready for use.

Temperature Control

Do not store above 50°C, or 122°F. Cylinder temperatures should not exceed 52°C, or 125°F.

Upright Storage

Store cylinders upright with valve-protection caps in place.

Secure Cylinders

Secure cylinders to prevent them from falling or being knocked over.

Physical Protection

Do not drag, roll, slide, or drop cylinders. Use a suitable hand truck when moving them.
