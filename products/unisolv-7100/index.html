Unisolv-7100

High-Performance HFE Cleaning Fluid

Unisolv-7100 is a hydrofluoroether (HFE) specialty fluid developed for precision cleaning, vapor degreasing, and electronics manufacturing applications. Designed as a replacement for 3M™ Novec™ 7100, it delivers reliable cleaning performance while supporting modern manufacturing requirements.

Product Overview

Unisolv-7100 is formulated to remove oils, greases, particulates, flux residues, and manufacturing contaminants from precision components. Its nonflammable chemistry, low viscosity, and favorable evaporation characteristics make it well suited for demanding cleaning applications in electronics and precision manufacturing.

Precision Cleaning

Designed for cleaning sensitive components while helping maintain consistent product quality.

Nonflammable

Low Residue

Fast evaporation characteristics help minimize residue following the cleaning process.
