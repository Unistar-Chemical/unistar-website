Unisolv-7200

High-Performance HFE Precision Cleaning Fluid

Unisolv-7200 is a high-purity hydrofluoroether specialty fluid developed for precision cleaning, industrial cleaning, and manufacturing applications. Designed as an alternative to 3M™ Novec™ 7200, it provides reliable performance for controlled cleaning processes and specialty fluid applications.

Product Overview

Unisolv-7200 is a transparent, colorless liquid containing at least 99 percent ethyl nonafluoroisobutyl ether. Its low viscosity, limited water solubility, and controlled evaporation characteristics make it suitable for evaluation in demanding industrial and precision-cleaning processes.

The SDS reports no closed-cup flash point; however, vapor-air mixtures may become combustible or explosive under certain conditions. Appropriate ventilation, ignition control, and handling procedures should always be followed.

Replacement Information

Unisolv-7200 is positioned as an alternative for applications currently using 3M™ Novec™ 7200. Product suitability should be confirmed through application testing, materials-compatibility review, process evaluation, and consultation with the appropriate technical personnel.

Safety and Handling Information

The SDS classifies Unisolv-7200 as potentially harmful if swallowed or in contact with skin. Use only for industrial or professional applications and maintain adequate workplace ventilation.

Keep the product away from sparks, flames, heat sources, strong acids, strong bases, and strong oxidizing agents. When exposure assessment indicates a need, use appropriate gloves, protective clothing, and respiratory protection.
