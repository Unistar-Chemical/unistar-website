Unisolv-7300

Versatile HFE Fluid for Cleaning and Thermal Applications

Unisolv-7300 is a high-purity specialty fluid developed for precision cleaning, electronic applications, cooling, and heat transfer processes. Designed as an alternative to 3M™ Novec™ 7300, it offers a versatile solution for applications requiring reliable solvent performance and thermal-management capabilities.

Product Overview

Unisolv-7300 is a specialty fluorinated fluid containing at least 99 percent of its primary chemical component. The Safety Data Sheet identifies its intended uses as a cooling agent, solvent, cleaning agent, and heat-transfer liquid.

Its nonflammable nature and application versatility make it suitable for evaluation across controlled precision-cleaning, electronics, cooling, and thermal-management processes. Product suitability should always be confirmed through process testing and compatibility review.

Replacement Information

Unisolv-7300 is positioned as an alternative for applications currently using 3M™ Novec™ 7300. Because operating requirements vary, customers should evaluate materials compatibility, equipment design, temperature conditions, cleaning requirements, and heat-transfer performance before making a transition.

Our team can assist with documentation, product selection, and application-evaluation questions.

Safety and Handling Information

Store Unisolv-7300 in a well-ventilated location with the container tightly closed. Keep it away from heat, acids, strong bases, and oxidizing agents.

Use appropriate ventilation to control workplace exposure. Gloves, protective clothing, and respiratory protection should be selected according to the conditions of use and the results of a workplace exposure assessment.
