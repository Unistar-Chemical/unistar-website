Unisolv-FC

Nonflammable Specialty Fluid for Fire Suppression and Heat-Transfer Applications

Unisolv-FC is a high-purity fluorinated ketone specialty fluid developed for use in fire-extinguishing systems, heat-transfer and cooling applications, specialty cleaning processes, and industrial solvent applications. Its nonflammable properties, low boiling point, and clean evaporation characteristics make it suitable for controlled systems requiring reliable performance and minimal residue.

Product Overview

Unisolv-FC is a colorless fluorinated ketone fluid designed for applications that require nonflammable performance, rapid evaporation, and compatibility with specialized industrial systems. The product may be used as an extinguishing agent, heat-transfer or cooling liquid, cleaning agent, and specialty solvent.

With a boiling point of 49.2°C and a density of 1.60 g/mL, Unisolv-FC supports efficient distribution and evaporation in properly engineered systems. The fluid is practically insoluble in water and remains stable under recommended storage conditions.

Safety and Handling

Unisolv-FC should be handled in accordance with good industrial hygiene and safety practices. Use adequate ventilation, especially in confined areas, and avoid breathing vapors, mist, or spray. Appropriate protective gloves, eye protection, and protective clothing should be used during handling.

Store the product in a tightly closed container in a dry, well-ventilated location. Consult the current Safety Data Sheet before handling, storing, transporting, or disposing of this product.
