Unisolv-YD

Low-GWP Hydrofluoroolefin Specialty Fluid

Unisolv-YD is a hydrofluoroolefin specialty fluid developed for industrial applications requiring low global-warming potential, nonflammable performance, and controlled evaporation. It is a clear, colorless liquid composed of (E)-1-chloro-2,3,3-trifluoropropene.

Product Overview

Unisolv-YD is a clear, colorless hydrofluoroolefin liquid with a weak odor and a boiling point of 54°C. The product is nonflammable and has a reported global-warming potential of 1 and ozone-depletion potential of 0.00002.

Its physical properties make it suitable for evaluation in selected industrial applications requiring a low-GWP specialty fluid. Suitability should be confirmed through process testing, materials-compatibility review, and application-specific evaluation.

Environmental Profile

Unisolv-YD has a reported global-warming potential of 1 and an ozone-depletion potential of 0.00002. It is also classified as harmful to aquatic life, so release into drains, waterways, soil, or the surrounding environment should be prevented.

Waste fluid, cleaning water, and empty containers should be handled through an appropriately licensed industrial waste-disposal contractor in accordance with applicable regulations.

Safety and Handling Information

Unisolv-YD may cause drowsiness or dizziness. Avoid breathing vapors, mists, or sprays and use only outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Vapor is heavier than air and may accumulate near floor level or in confined spaces.

Wear suitable protective gloves, protective clothing, and eye or face protection. Keep containers tightly closed, away from direct sunlight, sparks, flames, heat sources, strong acids, strong bases, oxidizing agents, and reactive metals.
